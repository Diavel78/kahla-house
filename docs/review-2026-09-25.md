## 4. Findings — ranked

Severity is about money first, then rent, then time. Line references are to
`app.py` at this commit unless stated.

### A. Pair engine

1. **HIGH, FIXED — the held leg's exit floor became a profit demand.** After a
   partner sells, the held leg floored at `cap − sold`. That dates from the cap
   being a value estimate (~100 + worth) where cap − sold ≈ cost. With the cap a
   120 loss budget it became cost + 5–11¢: CIN@PIT held away −2.5 at 58 and
   asked 65.5; LAC@BUF asked 64.5 on a 53 lot; WF@LOU 64.5 on 53.5; LV@NO 66 on
   54.3; IOWA@MICH 66 on 55; MIZ@MSST 61.5 on 52.5. Ten held legs parked well
   over the touch: no fill, no ask-side rent. The floor is now FLAT (pair cost −
   sold price; `sold_cost` is remembered at the sale), then this leg's own cost,
   and only without either the old formula. `_pair_plan`.
2. **HIGH, FIXED — the re-rung gave up after one option.** `_pair_rerung` tried
   `opts[0]` only; no slug / same rung / no rent → whole re-rung declined. It
   now walks the ladder to the first rung that has a slug and pays.
3. **HIGH, FIXED — amend shrank partially filled bids.** The venue's `modify`
   `quantity` is the order TOTAL with fills carried (the sell arm measured this
   Sep 9). `_pair_order_write` amended with `n = qty − held`; on a 15-lot leg
   holding 10.8 that made the order a 4-lot with nothing left to fill (or the
   venue rejected it and the bid never moved). Same bug in the repeg chase and
   the buy sniper (see C1). Now `n + cum`.
4. **HIGH, FIXED — one failed rent read cancelled both bids.** `_rent_ok` returns
   False on an unreadable venue answer (correct for placement) and the pair
   read that as `rent_pulled`, cancelled both bids, and re-placed them 10 min
   later at the back of the queue. Rent is now tri-state in the pair
   (`_pair_rent`); unreadable = keep the orders, like an unreadable book.
5. **MED, FIXED — create/cancel churn on unreachable pairs.** After "nothing
   reachable → cancel", the next tick re-created the same capped bid, and the
   120s re-rung clock cancelled it again: two writes a minute per pair, never
   at the touch. A capped bid now stays parked 10 min after an off-touch cancel.
6. **MED, FIXED — the $13 Master Rule was applied to ASKS.** A 19–20 lot held
   over ~65¢ could never list its exit (CAR@CLE over 41.5, live this morning).
   Bids only now.
7. **MED, FIXED — a vanished bid could seat a second full lot.** Positions come
   from the mirror (600s TTL); if the private socket blinked through a fill,
   the next tick saw no bid, h=0, and created a fresh 15. Now: a bid we created
   <20s ago is assumed still landing; a bid that vanished otherwise triggers a
   fresh positions read before any create.
8. **MED, FIXED — the pair's watch-list push evicted every other order slug.**
   `_pair_tick` pushed its ~50 slugs with `replace=True`, the repeg lap pushed
   its full set the same way, so the core/depth groups rebuilt every lap and
   the Ferrari's slugs were off the depth socket between pushes (REST book
   reads for snipers and fill status). The pair now MERGES.
9. **MED, FIXED — no lap budget, no rotation, silent slug cap.** `_pair_tick`
   walked every row with no clock (each amend sleeps 2s); `orders.list` takes
   400 slugs and pairs past #200 read `orders=[]` and created every tick.
   150s budget with fair rotation; uncovered rows are named, not quoted blind.
10. **LOW, FIXED — dead code.** `_pair_partner_options` was defined twice (the
    first, worth-capped version was shadowed). Deleted.
11. **OPEN — the pair lane wakes on every LITE frame of every watched market**
    (`wsfeed.py` ~1623, 10s cooldown), so "45s poll" is really ~10s; each tick
    is an `orders.list` + a `pair_hedges` write per row. Scope wakes to the
    lane's own dirty slugs (the set exists).
12. **OPEN — `_pair_step` returns from inside the leg loop on a foreign slug,**
    discarding leg-a lot memory when leg b is pick-owned.
13. **OPEN — the seeder prices the whole unpaired board every 5 min with no
    budget** and never consults its own `pair_declined` rows before pricing.
14. **OPEN — MLB totals have never produced a pair.** The path exists; the
    board/tape/candidates chain yields nothing. Worth one dry run with the
    seed log on before NHL, which will use the same non-football path.

### B. Ferrari (football executor) + OMS

1. **HIGH, FIXED — the recenter cancelled paying seats into a walled executor.**
   `pairs_own_football` (default on since Sep 21) makes `_gridiron_try_bet`
   return `pairs_own` for every football spread/total. `_gridiron_recenter_tick`
   did not know: an illegal-but-resting seat was cancelled, its pick deleted,
   its OMS row flipped pending — and the executor refused to re-seat it. Up to
   6 paying bids an hour gone for good. The recenter now stands down behind the
   wall (`gate: pairs_own`).
2. **MED, FIXED — the OMS paid full pricing to hear "no".** Every enrolled
   spread/total row was re-priced (projection + ladder pricing, up to 8 book
   fills or a 15–20s REST event search) every 30 min for a guaranteed refusal.
   The wall is now checked BEFORE pricing and retried every 4h. Same for the
   week-of bet sweep's dossier builds.
3. **HIGH, OPEN (dormant behind the wall) — the virgin seeder prices a stub's
   mirrored side at ~50¢.** `_gridiron_seed_virgin` pegs off `quote.bid` with no
   placeholder guard; the synthetic side of a 1/51 stub reads bid 49 → seat at
   49.5–50¢, chased to 60. Bites the moment `pairs_own_football` is flipped off
   with `seed_quotes` on.
4. **HIGH, OPEN — "one order per slug" is not enforced at `_autobet_execute`.**
   It guards positions, not resting orders; the football leg passes
   `skip_game_dedup=True`; a `duplicate key` on the pick insert is treated as
   "booked" and returns `placed`. Two lanes (OMS executor, seat top-up group
   pass) can compute the same rung and both create. The top-up's per-slug path
   does the fresh `orders.list` guard — the executor should too.
5. **MED, OPEN — the executor and the recenter feed `_gridiron_line_rule`
   different ladders** (paying+booked vs every non-placeholder rung), so a seat
   legal at placement can be "illegal" an hour later in ladder-center mode.
6. **MED, OPEN — the $20 event cap counts nominal stakes** (a 0.3 dust seat
   counts 20), so the "always 20 combined" top-up trips `event_cap` whenever
   both rungs were seated.
7. **MED, OPEN — a scissor is reachable when the model sits on the line**
   (`value_side is None` lets both sides through; each rung picks its own
   max-edge side).
8. **MED, OPEN — MLB seat top-up bypasses the book-exposure cap, the model wall
   and `_rent_dead`.**
9. **MED, OPEN — the CFB/UFC moneyline rent lane has no model wall anywhere**
   (chase, sniper and placement all exempt "gridiron"); a 40% favorite is
   chased to 60¢.
10. **MED, OPEN — no kickoff floor in the OMS executor** (`.gt(event_start, now)`
    only); a fresh 20-lot can seat minutes before kickoff, against the 60-min
    no-rebuy rule the top-up and recenter honor.
11. **LOW, OPEN — rent reads are unmetered** (`/v1/incentives` on the 30s

### C. Sell arm / repeg / snipers / reconcile

1. **HIGH, FIXED — buy-side amends sent `quantity = leaves`.** Repeg chase
   (`qty = int(leaves)` → `_repeg_amend`) and the buy sniper (`snap["qty"]`)
   shrank every partially filled bid by its fill on each amend: a 20-lot
   nibbled 0.3 went 19, 18, 17…; a real partial (cum 5) went 15 → 10 → 5 → 0 in
   three chases. The seat top-up cannot heal it (it only fires when NO bid
   rests). Both now send `cum + leaves`; the snapshot carries `cum`.
2. **HIGH, FIXED — the scalp read pending picks with `.limit(300)` and no
   ORDER BY.** PostgREST returns physical order; on a 390-pick Saturday, picks
   past row 300 never got an ask — naked inventory on the busiest days. Now
   paged (`_sb_paged`, ordered by event_start). Same shape, OPEN: reconcile
   (`.limit(400)`), recenter (`.limit(400)`), rent-unpaid / rent-cull / autolog
   at `.limit(1000)`.
3. **MED, FIXED — the buy sniper's pop guard was a literal `pass`.** A fill
   landing mid-lap popped `BUY_SNAP`; the publish re-inserted pre-fill leaves
   and the sniper could amend the order to a stale total. The guard now compares
   against the lap's actual venue-read time (`fs["read_mono"]`).
4. **MED, OPEN — the lot-ledger clamp under-counts fresh fills** (240s TTL on
   top of the activities lag): a 15-contract top-up fill on a 5-lot slug reads
   as 5 for up to 9 min; the fast ask refuses to help ("one ask already");

5. **MED, OPEN — `_scalp_amend` "gone" → fresh create at lap-start `held`** with

6. **MED, OPEN — the amend-path re-list adopts MANUAL asks as ours** (hardcoded

7. **MED, OPEN — mirror write ordering:** a fresh REST read replaces the whole

## 5. Decisions I made (each is one flag to reverse)

- **Pair seeding lead follows the rent program.** `_pair_min_lead_h`: 6h while
  the family pays early (your Sep 20 rule), 3h when only day-of pays
  (machine_flags `pair_min_lead_dayof_h`); `pair_min_lead_h` overrides both.
  Rationale: rule #1 runs forward — the paying window is T-6h → kickoff, and
  the T-30 cancel already protects an unpaired pair. To restore the old
  behavior: `insert into machine_flags(key,value) values('pair_min_lead_h','6')`.

  back plus rent"). The spec table said cap − sold; it was written when cap ≈
  value. Reverse by restoring the two lines in `_pair_plan` (marked).
- **The $13 Master Rule no longer applies to pair asks.** Selling a held lot
  adds no exposure.
- **`pairs_own_football` stays as you set it.** I only made the rest of the
  machine (OMS, recenter, sweep) respect it before spending money or cancels.

**Speed**
