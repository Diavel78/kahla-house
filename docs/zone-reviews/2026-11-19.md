*By dog size:* follow-the-money on a short dog (+0.5 to +4.5) **1-7**; +5 to +9.5 5-3; +10 to +14 2-3; +14.5 up 13-16.
Nobody-moved dog: short dogs 15-11 (+10%), +5 to +14 10-12, +14.5 up 10-9. The Nov 15 "every dog +14.5 or more
lost" note did not hold up (big dogs ~even).
*By slot (ET start):* nobody-moved dog — before 7 PM 17-11 (+16%), 7 PM+ 18-21 (−12%); follow — 7 PM+ 18-22.
*Home vs road:* nobody-moved **road dog 31-20 (+16%), home dog 4-12 (−52%)**; follow — road 19-26, home 2-3.
*By day:* follow lost every day (1-1, 4-9, 9-10, 7-9).
*Totals:* Friday unders hasn't had a Friday yet (Nov 20 is the first). Every other day's under lost overall
(Sun 18-10, Mon 10-12, Tue 21-30, Wed 12-30) — no reason to widen the spot.

**Is ~40-60 a rule enough?** To tell a real 55% from a coin flip you need ~250-400 bets; at 50 the 95%
band on the win rate is ±14 points. So no split here is proof. But it is enough to say when a rule's
whole premise failed at the close, and that is the case: the measured basis was an ESPN/DK open→close
regime (+6% for follow, the weakest of the three when it was built), and our own Pinnacle tape says
21-29 in its first four days.

**What a measured NCAAB zone needs** (zone_review's rule): |r| ≥ 0.15 over ≥ 60 game-markets, then ≥ 20 bets
at the threshold, ROI ≥ +10%, z ≥ 1.5, and the next threshold down positive. NCAAB has 146 a market now, so
three windows already qualify on paper — spread last hour chase 15-7 (+30%), totals 1-6h after open chase
31-17 (+22%), totals 6-3h before chase 19-8 (+33%). All of it is four days of one regime. The runbook's
"two weeks behind a new zone" caution applies; and the totals chase contradicts three seasons of ESPN
open→close data (no totals move rule won). Recommend: watch them one more week before they go live.
