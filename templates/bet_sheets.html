One line, every book: tap a line above and every book is priced at it, in cents for the side you pick (cents = the chance it wins). Each line is that book's bid. Solid = that book lists the line. Dashed = it doesn't, so its price is carried from its nearest lines by how far it sits from Pinnacle on those lines. Pinnacle fair = Pinnacle's price with the vig taken out, off its own alternate lines.
