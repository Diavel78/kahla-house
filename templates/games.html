How scoring works
  • 17 hands: deal 7,6,5,4,3,2,1, then three middle hands of 7 cards (No Trump, Blind Diamonds, Negative Spades), then 1,2,3,4,5,6,7 back up.
  • Trump cycles ♣ ♦ ♥ ♠ continuously through the up-and-down hands. Blind Diamonds = you bid before seeing your hand.
  • Make your bid exactly → bid × 11. A made 0 bid scores 10. Miss your bid → you score the number of tricks you took.
  • Negative Spades: spades are trump and every trick you take is −10.
  • Each player may bid 0 at most 3 times per game. Total bids on a hand can't equal the cards dealt (screw-the-dealer).
